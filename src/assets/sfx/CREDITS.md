# Sound credits

Every sound effect, jingle and music loop in this folder is cut, layered, levelled and re-encoded
(`<id>-<n>.ogg` + `.mp3`, mono 44.1 kHz; `music/*.ogg|.mp3`, stereo loops) from **real recordings and
instrument recordings released under CC0 1.0 (public domain)**: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>.
CC0 needs no attribution; the sources are listed so every file can be traced. The edited files here are
released under CC0 as well.

The meme-style sounds (vine boom, airhorn, bruh, oof, wow, record scratch, sad trombone...) are **not** rips
of the famous clips: they are CC0 recordings of the same kind of thing (an air horn, a trombone, a person
saying "bruh"), and the "vine boom" is our own layer of CC0 booms + a sub drop + a reverb tail.

Copies were fetched from public GitHub repositories that ship these CC0 files with per-file provenance
(each listed repo states the CC0 licence and links the original Freesound page or Kenney pack).
Built by a scratch script (trim, fades, layering, pitch/tempo, small reverb, loudness levelling).
